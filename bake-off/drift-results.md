# Drift test (issue #1240 follow-up)

Run: 2026-10-12.
Script: `scripts/bake-off-drift-test.mts` (3 identical runs per slot,
fixed seed=42, prompt from `bake-off/vacant-insert.json`).

Diff is mean RGB delta in the **mask-complement region** — i.e. every pixel
*outside* the white edit rectangle. The white rect is the edit area per fal's
mask convention (white = edit, black = preserve), and OpenAI's convention
(alpha=0 = edit, alpha=255 = preserve) is converted in
`scripts/bake-off-mask-invert.mts`. Lower = more stable.

## Correction

Earlier versions of this file (Oct 12, before this run) had bake-off
results that inverted the mask convention — the masks sent to fal were
black-rect-on-white, but fal interprets **white = edit, black = preserve**.
That means every "edited" image we produced was actually generated by
editing the *outside* of the rectangle and preserving the rectangle —
the opposite of what we wanted. Drift numbers from that earlier run were
also measuring the wrong region (skipping the rect that was actually
preserved).

The fixed version of `scripts/bake-off-prep-masks.mts` now produces
white-rect-on-black PNGs. The fixed `scripts/bake-off-mask-invert.mts`
now correctly converts white pixels to alpha=0 for OpenAI's edits endpoint.

## Finding

All three **mask-capable** slots (FLUX Pro Fill, Z-Image Turbo Inpaint,
FLUX Kontext LoRA Inpaint) preserve the unedited region essentially
perfectly — mean RGB delta well under 1, max delta at most 8/255 across
~700k-1.85M pixels. **There is no drift difference between FLUX and the
two challengers.** Earlier claims that FLUX was uniquely stable were
artifacts of the inverted mask.

Both **Seedream variants** continue to drift badly on fal's queue
endpoint — they take no `mask_url` and re-render the whole frame. The
Picsart article's "Pro and Flash preserve the unedited region" claim is
a UI feature (in-image sketch annotations), not an API parameter. No
vendor surface (fal, Picsart, Volcano Engine/BytePlus) exposes a real mask
for Seedream.

## Implication

The mask-fill candidates are now a fair three-way bake-off. Z-Image Turbo
is the standout on cost ($0.01/MP, 5× cheaper than FLUX) and speed (~8s).
The three models should be scored on subjective quality, not drift.

## Recommendation

Re-run the visual bake-off with corrected masks. Seedream is out
(documented above). The three-way comparison is FLUX Pro Fill (incumbent)
vs Z-Image Turbo Inpaint (cheapest, newest) vs FLUX Kontext LoRA Inpaint
(Kontext family at $0.035/MP).
